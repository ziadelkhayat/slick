Business day

Inventory transactions after this time are added to the next business day. Empty = same as the business day start.
At the start time, if the previous day wasn't ended manually and all its orders are closed, the branch POS ends it automatically.
Sales before this time count on the previous business day. Set it after your closing time, e.g. 03:00 if you close at 2 AM. Must be between 12:00 AM and 11:59 AM.

Opening hours

Times after midnight (e.g. closes 02:00) count for the day that opened.
